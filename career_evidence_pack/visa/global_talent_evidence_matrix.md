## Digital Technology Criteria Mapping

UK Global Talent digital technology guidance includes exceptional talent and exceptional promise routes. Public guidance describes evidence such as innovation, product-led technology contributions, contributions outside work, and research publication or expert endorsement.

Potential evidence mapping:

- Innovation as founder / product builder: CompressAI semantic compression platform
- Innovation in a new digital technology field: semantic communication for Earth Observation
- Contributions outside work: open-source repository, technical reports, benchmark framework
- Research published or endorsed by expert: future arXiv preprint, supervisor letters, workshop submission

## Recommended 90-Day Evidence Plan

- finalise public GitHub README

- collect written expert feedback
