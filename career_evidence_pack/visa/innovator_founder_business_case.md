# Innovator Founder Business Case

## Business Name Placeholder

Orbital Semantic Compression Ltd

## One-Line Business Proposition

Onboard AI software that reduces satellite downlink bandwidth while preserving mission-critical Earth Observation information.

## Problem

Earth Observation satellites collect large volumes of imagery, but downlink capacity, contact windows, power budgets, and latency limit how much information can be transmitted quickly. During wildfire monitoring and other emergency missions, transmitting visually complete imagery is less important than preserving mission-critical information for decision making.

## Proposed Product

A semantic communication software layer for satellites and edge Earth Observation systems. The product analyses imagery onboard, estimates mission utility, encodes images into learned visual tokens, and prioritises transmission of the most relevant tokens under bandwidth constraints.

Initial mission focus:

- wildfire monitoring
- smoke and burn-scar relevance
- emergency Earth Observation

Future applications:

- flood assessment
- maritime surveillance
- infrastructure monitoring
- defence and civil protection

## Innovation

The innovation is not ordinary image compression. The innovation is a mission-aware semantic transmission layer that optimises what is sent according to downstream Earth Observation utility.

Current technical evidence shows:

- modular VQ-VAE token compression
- wildfire utility analysis
- utility-aware token selection
- Sentinel-2 benchmark validation
- satellite communication metrics
- statistical testing

This directly supports the Innovator Founder requirement that a business idea should be innovative, viable, and scalable.

## Viability

The near-term product can be developed as a software layer for research organisations, CubeSat teams, and Earth Observation analytics companies.
