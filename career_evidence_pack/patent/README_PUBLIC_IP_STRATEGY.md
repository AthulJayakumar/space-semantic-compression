# Public-Safe IP Strategy

## Working Invention Title

Semantic Utility-Aware Token Transmission for Resource-Constrained Earth Observation Satellites

## Public-Safe Description

The project investigates whether satellite imagery can be compressed and transmitted according to mission relevance rather than pixel-level distortion alone. The current wildfire-focused implementation uses semantic utility maps, learned token representations, and bandwidth-aware token retention to evaluate whether wildfire-relevant information can be preserved under severe communication constraints.

This public description is intentionally high level. Do not include unpublished claim language, implementation-specific decision rules, or detailed technical mechanisms in public material before patent advice.

## Patent-Relevant Technical Direction

The strongest patent direction is not generic AI compression. A stronger and more defensible direction is:

> Mission-utility-aware semantic token selection and adaptive transmission for onboard Earth Observation systems.

Potentially protectable areas to discuss with a patent attorney:

- generation of mission-specific utility maps for onboard satellite imagery
- mapping utility estimates onto learned token representations
- token ranking according to semantic utility and communication constraints
- adaptive transmission under bandwidth, latency, packet loss, or downlink availability constraints
- mission-specific reconstruction evaluation using downstream detector retention
- onboard wildfire-focused semantic communication pipeline for Earth Observation satellites

## Why This May Be Industrially Relevant

The technology has a practical engineering target: reducing satellite downlink load while preserving mission-critical information. Potential users include:

- CubeSat operators
- Earth Observation analytics providers
- wildfire monitoring agencies
- environmental monitoring companies
- defence and civil protection organisations
- edge AI satellite payload teams

## What To Keep Private

Keep the following private until legal review:

- exact claim drafts
- detailed algorithmic variants not already public
- unpublished training and selection formulas
- unreleased detector-retention-aware training objectives
- commercial roadmap linked to specific claims
- invention disclosure documents
- prior-art comparison notes

## Patent Attorney Questions

Ask a patent attorney:

1. Is the invention best framed as a technical process for satellite communication rather than software alone?
2. Which parts should be patented versus kept as trade secrets?
3. Does the existing public GitHub disclosure limit claim scope?
4. Can claims focus on onboard edge processing and adaptive downlink behaviour?
5. Should the filing route be UK-first, EPO, or PCT?
6. What must be removed from public talks, GitHub, and preprints before filing?

## Official Guidance Checked

- UK patents require the invention to be new, inventive, and capable of being made or used as a technical process or method.
- UK guidance warns that public disclosure before filing can harm patentability.
- UK guidance recommends professional help for patent drafting and use of NDAs for confidential discussion.
