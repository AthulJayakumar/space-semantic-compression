## Required corrections

1. **Abstract, final paragraph:** Replace the claim that DFire, FLAME and the 100-scene CEMS benchmark jointly indicate high-utility transmission at very high savings. That sentence is not a matched-rate superiority result, and 52/100 previously evaluated ImpactMesh tiles have reconstructable event-code overlap with known checkpoint inputs. The lineage audit itself is conditional because an ancestor manifest is missing. A defensible replacement is: "A working VQ-VAE semantic-transmission prototype and preliminary wildfire experiments motivate the study. In a frozen 1,200-byte Sentinel-2 validation comparison over 60 burn/negative event pairs, a training-adapted VQ-VAE improved over its base checkpoint, but JPEG2000 retained higher SUS and dNBR-proxy Dice. The PhD will establish whether this gap can be closed under stronger independent-label and geographic validation."
2. **Section 8, Preliminary Experimental Evidence:** Keep the historical DFire/FLAME and CEMS experiments only as exploratory platform demonstrations, clearly separated from current evidence. Replace the final two CEMS paragraphs as the principal satellite claim with the frozen official-validation table: JPEG2000 95.12 SUS / 0.533 proxy Dice; adapted VQ-VAE 83.28 / 0.469; base VQ-VAE 70.85 / 0.286 at a common 1,200-byte cap. State the +12.43 SUS and +0.183 Dice paired adaptation gains, but also the adapted-minus-JPEG2000 deficits and +0.284 negative predicted-positive area increase. Cite `VALIDATION_REPORT.md` and state that labels are quantised dNBR proxies and countries overlap training. Do not call this independent confirmation or publication-grade superiority.
3. **Sections 9-10, baseline and evaluation language:** Distinguish an equal maximum payload from exactly equal transmitted bytes. JPEG/JPEG2000 chose the best original-image PSNR among predefined feasible settings. Report actual serialised bytes and codec-selection assumptions. Do not imply LPIPS, onboard energy or realistic downlink savings were measured in the 60-event comparison; they were not. Treat CCSDS as a planned baseline, not an existing measured result.
4. **Advancement and test status:** State that the predeclared 18-event joint SUS plus proxy-Dice development gate failed and was subsequently reused, so it is exploratory. The 37-pair geographically screened EcoFireBias test cohort is sealed and must remain unscored unless a genuinely new predeclared training/development advancement path is met. The 60-event official validation split has now been consulted once and must not be reused for iterative model selection.
5. **Reproducibility claim:** Make clear that patch-derived event IDs do not establish scene-footprint independence and the inherited checkpoint lineage is incompletely reconstructable. A future claim of independent satellite validation requires source scene IDs, geospatial footprints, license, mask provenance, no train/test event leakage, and negative scenes.

## Application-safe positioning

"I have built a reproducible semantic-transmission prototype and tested its failure modes. Adaptation improved the learned baseline on a frozen validation cohort, but a conventional codec remains stronger at the tested byte ceiling. My PhD question is therefore sharp and falsifiable: when, if ever, can wildfire utility-aware token transmission beat strong classical baselines without increasing false alarms, under independently verified Earth Observation labels and realistic onboard constraints?"

This framing supports a PhD application because it demonstrates a working research platform, quantitative preliminary evidence, a strong comparator, and a bounded path to decisive experiments. It does **not** establish novelty, operational advantage, or publication acceptance by itself.
